# Food and local experiences

Hawker centres are central to Singapore's everyday food culture and allow travellers to sample dishes from different culinary traditions in one place. Suitable well-known areas include Maxwell Food Centre near Chinatown, Lau Pa Sat in the central business district and Tekka Centre in Little India. Availability and opening times vary by stall.

Common dishes visitors may explore include chicken rice, laksa, satay, roti prata, kaya toast and chilli crab. Travellers with dietary restrictions should ask vendors about ingredients because recipes vary. Hawker dining is generally informal: return trays where required, share tables considerately and avoid occupying seats longer than necessary during peak periods.

Local-experience ideas include an early visit to a neighbourhood market, a guided heritage walk, a cooking class, sampling coffee and kaya toast, exploring independent businesses in heritage districts, or visiting a hawker centre with a small tasting budget. Food activities are convenient indoor or sheltered alternatives during a short rain period, although travel between venues may still be exposed.
