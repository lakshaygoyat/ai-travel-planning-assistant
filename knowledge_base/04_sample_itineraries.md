# Sample itinerary patterns

A balanced first three-day visit can use geographic grouping rather than attempting every attraction.

Day 1 — Marina Bay: begin around the Civic District or Merlion waterfront, continue to Gardens by the Bay, and use the climate-controlled conservatories as an indoor alternative. Finish with an evening waterfront walk if conditions are comfortable.

Day 2 — Cultural districts: explore Chinatown in the morning, including heritage streets and a food stop. Continue to Kampong Glam and, if time and energy allow, Little India. Religious and cultural sites require respectful behaviour and dress. Museums can replace exposed walking during persistent rain.

Day 3 — Choose by traveller profile. Families may select Sentosa or the Mandai wildlife parks. Garden enthusiasts may visit the Botanic Gardens and National Orchid Garden. Museum-focused travellers can combine the National Museum or Asian Civilisations Museum with sheltered dining and shopping.

Avoid overloading each day. Reserve time for meals, public-transport transfers and rest in the heat. Put outdoor gardens and neighbourhood walks into the driest forecast windows; keep museums, conservatories, Jewel Changi Airport or other indoor attractions as alternatives. Verify opening times, maintenance closures and ticket requirements on official sites.
